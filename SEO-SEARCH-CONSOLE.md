## 4) What NOT to do (slows you down)

- Don’t host a full duplicate site on `boonbuys.com` — it splits SEO
- Don’t buy random spam backlinks
- Don’t chase only the keyword `boonbuy` vs official `boonbuy.com`
- Don’t change domain / brand name every week
